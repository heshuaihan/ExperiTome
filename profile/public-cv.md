**Public CV · 2026-10-24**

## Profile / 个人简介

Shuaihan He is a PhD student in the Bioscience and Biomedical Engineering Thrust and Brain and Intelligence Research Institute at The Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (Guangzhou). His interests include population genetics and indigenous pig resources, computational proteomics and multi-omics integration, and molecular mechanisms related to brain aging.

何帅涵现为香港科技大学（广州）生命科学与生物医学工程学域、脑与智能研究所博士生。研究兴趣包括群体遗传与地方猪遗传资源、计算蛋白质组学与多组学整合，以及脑衰老相关的分子机制。

## Research interests / 研究兴趣

- Population genetics, genetic variation, and indigenous pig resources / 群体遗传、遗传变异与地方猪遗传资源
- Computational proteomics and integrative multi-omics analysis / 计算蛋白质组学与多组学整合分析
- Synaptic plasticity and molecular mechanisms related to brain aging / 脑衰老相关的突触可塑性与分子机制
